How the radar prices a launch

Implied value = base value × momentum × maturity × breadth. The base value is chosen by validation, not by assertion: three candidate pricings of a protocol's TVL — the category's median tokened market-cap-to-TVL multiple, a log-log regression of market cap on TVL fitted per category group, and the geometric blend of the two — are built on one half of the snapshot's tokened peers and scored on the held-out half, and the winner prices the board. Momentum bends the price for the week's TVL change, maturity adds a small capped premium for each year a protocol has run with no token, and breadth adds a mild term for multi-chain reach. Tokened protocols are never ranked here: the board separates them out and uses them only as the pricing peers.

An implied value is this entry's own estimate from DefiLlama figures in the sealed snapshot. It is not a prediction that a token will launch, or when, or what it would trade at. A protocol shown as recording no token may still have one its record does not carry.
